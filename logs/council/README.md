# Council of AI Decision Records (`logs/council/`)

This directory stores persistent Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) and multi-agent review logs generated by the Council of AI CLI runner (`rtk ./run.sh council "<topic>" --save`).

Based on *The Council of AI: A Multi-Agent Prompting Framework for Better Decision-Making* by Marius Silo ([Silotech.xyz](https://medium.com/@Silotech.xyz/the-council-of-ai-a-multi-agent-prompting-framework-for-better-decision-making-8e7569c10584)).

## Format
Files are named: `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` containing:
- 🔴 Round 1: Skeptic analysis (fatal flaws & invariants)
- 🟢 Round 2: Visionary analysis (10x multiplier & DX)
- 🟡 Round 3: Pragmatist analysis (Day-1 minimal testable slice)
- ⚖️ Round 4: Council Synthesis & Verdict (`GO` / `PIVOT` / `REJECT`)
