## 1. Functional Specification

When rendering autonomous Forge Apps inside the Portal canvas:

1. Micro-apps shall be embedded within strict HTML5 `<iframe>` elements configured with `sandbox="allow-scripts allow-forms allow-same-origin"`.
2. Cross-frame communication shall use bidirectional `window.postMessage` with strict origin verification (`event.origin === window.location.origin`).
3. Iframe sandboxes shall be prevented from accessing parent DOM, top-level cookie jars, or executing unauthorized top-level redirects.
